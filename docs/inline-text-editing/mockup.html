Release plan: reviewer edit loop

Draft 3, owned by the platform team

Summary

The review surface lets a reader point at a problem and describe it. Every fix still travels back through the agent, even when the fix is a single wrong word. This plan closes that gap for text.

We expect the median review to contain four or five small text corrections and one structural comment. Today all six of them cost a full agent round trip.

Scope for the first release

  • Replace a selected range of words with new text.
  • Mark a selected range, or a whole line, for deletion.
  • Save the collected edits into the artifact, or hand them to the agent.
  • This line is filler and should not survive the review.

Risks

The artifact is written by the agent. If the reviewer writes to the same file, the two writers can disagree. The plan keeps one owner per save and records who changed what.

Editing in place. ⌘Enter saves, Escape cancels.
